# Demo script — DubaiNow Next (about 5 minutes)

Start from the Welcome stories (`tools/build.sh run -welcome`, or sign out from Profile).

1. **Welcome.** Let two stories play (6 s each; hold to pause, tap the sides to skip).
   Tap **Sign in with UAE PASS** → the hand-off → **Choose a demo resident** → **Sara Haddad** → Face ID tick.
2. **Home (Sara).** "Needs you" shows 4 things, 2 due this week, and **Pay all · AED 1,012.60**.
   Swipe the cards (DEWA bill, passport upload, speeding fine, car registration). Fold Needs you with the chevron.
   Scroll: Favorites (the parking tile shows the live session), the suggested **Family visas** widget, live widgets
   (parking countdown, Salik, prayer, Kite Beach, metro, energy). Pull past the end → the widget gallery.
3. **The assistant.** Tap the logo at the top. Tap **Renew my car registration**: it sees the speeding fine blocking
   the renewal, clears it, prepares the renewal and asks for one confirm (**Pay AED 1,020**) → done with its receipt.
   Close: you're back on Home, already updated (Needs you drops from 4 to 2).
   (Do this before paying the fine any other way — afterwards the renewal is just AED 420.)
4. **Pay a bill.** On the DEWA card tap **Pay now** → check and pay → **Pay** (Face ID if enrolled in the Simulator:
   Features › Face ID) → the receipt with the Happiness faces → Done. (With 2+ payments due, **Pay all** pays them together.)
5. **Search.** Tap the search circle. Type `mulkia` (typo + local word) → "Renew your vehicle registration".
   Type `how much do I owe` → a direct answer from Sara's accounts.
6. **Services.** Browse by topic → Driving and transport → the personal banner and rows. Back on Services, scroll to
   **All at once** (bundles) and **Start to finish** (journeys); open **Build a house**. "All services, A to Z" lists all 219.
7. **Activity.** Needs you, In progress, Drafts, History. Open the visa renewal → **Scan passport** → Use this photo →
   the next step asks for the fees.
8. **Other residents.** Avatar → Profile → **Demo controls** → sign in as:
   - **Khalid** — the Build a house journey on Home (stage 4 of 7, review the villa drawings).
   - **Faisal** — Pay all for the café: DEWA, du and a van fine (AED 2,035.20).
   - **Wei** — tap the logo, choose the Mandarin suggestion: the assistant prepares a complaint about unpaid wages
     and asks for one UAE PASS confirm, in Mandarin (with "Show in English").
9. **Happiness Meter.** The smiley top left → one tap → reasons.

Demo controls also change the time of day (the Home wash: morning, midday, evening, night) and reset the data.
Nothing is real: no accounts, no payments, no government systems.
